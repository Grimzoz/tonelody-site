Support

Tonelody helps you learn harmonica and kalimba on your real instrument. Notes fall toward a line; you play them when they arrive. Slow the song down, loop a phrase, or let the song wait for you.

Contact

Questions, a wrong note in a song, or an idea? Write to endkeystudios@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

Please include your phone model and the song name if something sounds or looks wrong.

Frequently asked questions

I can't hear the notes.

Turn the volume up with the side buttons while a song is playing. Check that the reference tone is on (speaker button at the top of the player) and that its volume in Settings is not at zero.

The sound is late compared to the notes.

Bluetooth headphones add a delay. Open Settings › Advanced › Audio delay and tap along with the clicks; Tonelody measures the delay and corrects it.

Which instruments do I need?

A 10-hole diatonic harmonica in C, or a 17-key kalimba in C. Other keys are not supported yet.

Does Tonelody listen to me?

No. Tonelody does not use the microphone. It shows the notes and plays reference tones; you play along.

Does it cost anything?

No. Tonelody is free, with no ads and no account.
