Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Tonelody is developed by End Key Studios. This page explains how the app handles your information. In short: Tonelody does not collect any personal data.

No data collection

Tonelody has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, receive, share or sell any information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

Your settings — language, theme, the instrument you chose, the last speed of each song, audio delay and similar preferences — are saved on your device using the operating system's standard storage. They never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Microphone

Tonelody does not use the microphone.

Internet

All songs and sounds are part of the app. Tonelody does not need an internet connection to work and does not send anything to our servers.

Children

Tonelody can be used by people of all ages. Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example when a future version adds new features — the updated version will be published on this page before the change takes effect.
